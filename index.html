Why is Apple classified as a multinational corporation?

Apple Inc. is classified as a multinational corporation because it is headquartered in the United States while conducting business and generating sales across many countries and regions worldwide. Apple has a global market presence, with sales outside the United States accounting for the majority of its total net sales. Its operations cover major regions such as the Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, and the rest of Asia Pacific. Apple also relies on a global supply chain, with much of its manufacturing performed by outsourcing partners in countries including China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, and Vietnam.

Apple Facts

1976 Founding

Garage Beginnings

Founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in Jobs' family home garage in Los Altos, California, introducing the hand-crafted Apple I computer.

1984 Computing

The Macintosh GUI

In 1984, Apple pioneered mass-market personal computing with the Macintosh, popularizing graphical user interfaces, windowed applications, and the computer mouse.

2001 Digital Music

1,000 Songs in Your Pocket

The introduction of the original iPod and iTunes in 2001 transformed digital media consumption, paving the way for the mobile music and streaming era.

2007 Mobile Breakthrough

The Smartphone Standard

Steve Jobs unveiled the iPhone in 2007 as three revolutionary products in one device — a widescreen iPod, a mobile phone, and a breakthrough internet communicator.

2014 Health & Horology

The Apple Watch

Unveiled in 2014, the Apple Watch evolved into the world's most popular watch, saving countless lives through proactive ECG, Fall Detection, and Sleep Apnea notifications.
